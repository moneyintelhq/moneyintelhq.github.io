How to Analyse an Industry Before You Analyse a Company

A practical framework for understanding market structure, competition, regulation and the forces that shape industry economics.

By Money Intel EditorialSeptember 2026Money Intel

Map the industry

Identify customers, suppliers, competitors, substitutes and the major sources of demand. Then ask where the industry makes money and where value leaks out.

Look at industry economics

Study margins, capital intensity, working capital, pricing power and cyclicality. Attractive industries tend to have characteristics that allow strong businesses to earn healthy returns without continuously destroying capital.

Understand competitive structure

Ask whether scale matters, whether switching costs exist, how differentiated products are and how easy new competitors can enter. Industry structure shapes how durable a company’s advantage can be.

Then compare companies

Only after understanding the industry should you compare market share, growth, profitability, balance sheets and valuation. Context makes company-level numbers far more meaningful.
