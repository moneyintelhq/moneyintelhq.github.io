Privacy Policy

This page describes the current privacy practices of the Money Intel website.

When external services such as an email newsletter provider, analytics platform or advertising network are introduced, they may process information according to their own policies. Money Intel will update this page before those services are activated.

Contact and updates

We may update this policy when the website’s functionality changes. The effective version will be the one published on this page.
