About Money Intel

Practical money, investing, markets and career intelligence for India’s next generation.

What we publish

Money Intel explains financial concepts, investing frameworks, market developments and career decisions in clear language. The goal is to help readers understand the underlying idea rather than simply follow a headline.

How we approach editorial work

We aim to separate facts from interpretation, explain our reasoning, acknowledge uncertainty and make it clear when an idea is educational rather than a recommendation. Articles may evolve as new information becomes available.

Our standard

Useful content should give you something you can apply: a framework, a question to ask, a number to understand or a better way to evaluate a decision.
