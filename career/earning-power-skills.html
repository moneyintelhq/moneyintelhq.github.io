Skills That Can Increase Your Earning Power

Think about career skills as assets that can compound across roles, industries and years.

By Money Intel EditorialSeptember 2026Money Intel

Prioritise transferable skills

Communication, analytical thinking, writing, sales, project execution and comfort with technology can remain useful even when job titles change. Skills become more valuable when they solve expensive problems.

Pair a domain with a multiplier

Domain knowledge becomes more powerful when combined with a multiplier such as data analysis, automation, communication or commercial judgment. The combination can be harder to replace than either skill alone.

Build proof

A skill without evidence is difficult for an employer to price. Create case studies, projects, dashboards, writing samples or measurable work outcomes that make the skill visible.

Keep learning close to real work

Courses can help, but applying what you learn to a real business problem is usually a stronger signal. Aim to turn learning into an observable result.
