# Money Intel — SEO-ready static site

This version preserves the Money Intel visual identity while adding: unique page titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, favicon, article schema, real article URLs, improved internal linking, robots.txt and an updated sitemap.

## Deploy
Upload the contents of this folder to the root of the GitHub Pages repository. Keep the folder structure intact.

## Before launch
- Connect the newsletter form to a real email provider.
- Add the real editorial contact address to contact.html.
- Add Google Analytics only after choosing the measurement setup, then update privacy.html.
- Review legal pages for your jurisdiction and actual services.
- Replace starter article content with your final researched/edited versions as the publication grows.
